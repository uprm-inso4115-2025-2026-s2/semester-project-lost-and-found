==== Contextual Factors Affecting Report Interpretation
:author: Nelson Caban

===== Goal
Analyze how context (time, place, and situation) changes how reports are understood and used in the Lost & Found system.

===== Proposed Solution

====== Key Idea
A report does not always mean the same thing. Its meaning depends on context like location, time, and the person viewing it.

---

====== Contextual Factors

. Location
* Where the item was lost or found
* Affects how likely it is to recover

Example:
* Classroom - easier to find
* Public area - harder to find

---

. Time
* When the item was lost or reported
* How recent the report is

Example:
* Lost today - high chance of match
* Lost weeks ago - low chance

---

. Item Type
* What kind of item it is

Example:
* Phone - high priority
* Water bottle - low priority

---

. Environment
* What was happening around that time

Example:
* Big event - many similar reports
* Normal day - fewer reports

---

. User Perspective
* Different users see the report differently

Example:
* Claimant - wants item back
* Finder - trying to identify owner
* Admin - checking if claim is valid

---

====== Impact on Reports

Example Report:
"Black backpack lost near library at 3PM"

Different meanings:
* Claimant - urgent personal loss
* Finder - possible match
* Admin - needs verification (many similar items)

---

====== Scenarios

Scenario 1: Easy Match
* Unique item
* Clear location
* Recent time

* High confidence match

---

Scenario 2: Difficult Match
* Common item
* Vague location
* Unknown time

* Low confidence match

---

====== Conclusion

Context affects:
* How reports are understood
* How matches are made
* How admins make decisions

Reports should not be analyzed alone, but with their context.
