==== *Terminology*

This section defines key concepts within the lost-and-found domain. 
Each term is annotated with its *type* (entity, event, behavior, function) and the *phase* in which it is introduced. 

===== Type: Entity & Phase: Domain 

[col="1,3"]
|===
|Term |Definition

|*Lost Item*
|Personal belonging whose owner no longer knows its location and intends to recover it.

|*Found Item*
|Personal belonging discovered by someone other than its owner.

|*Owner*
|Individual who has legitimate claim over a lost item.

|*Finder*
|Individual who discovers a lost item.

|*Holder*
|A person or office temporarily responsible for keeping a found item until it can be returned.

|*Returner*
|A person facilitating the transfer of a found item back to its owner. 

|*Loss Location*
|Area where an item is believe to have been lost.

|*Discovery Location*
|Area where an item is found, which may differe from loss location.

|*Custody Location*
|The place where a found item is kept while awaiting return.

|*Holding Period*
|Duration during which a found item is kept before alternarive handling is considered. 

|*Unclaimed Item*
|A found item that has not been successfully returned to its owner within the holding period. 

|*Report*
|Recorded description of a lost or found item that supports identification. 
|===

===== Type: Event & Phase: Domain

[col="1,3"]
|===
|Term |Definition

|*Loss Realization*
|The moment an individual becomes aware that an item is missing.

|*Item Handed Over*
|The transfer of a found item to a holder or custody location.

|*Claim Attempt*
|An individual asserts ownership of a found item. 

|*Item Returned*
|The event in which a found item is successfully transferred back to its owner.

|*Holding Period Expired*
|The moment when a defined holding period ends without successful return.

|*Report Created*
|Documentation of a lost or found item. 

|*Report Closed*
|Conclusion of a report when no further action is expected.
|===
===== Type: Behavior & Phase: Domain

[col="1,3"]
|===
|Term |Definition

|*Item Handling*
|Behavior consisting of the actions and events involved in managing a found item, including a _Item Handed Over_, custody at a _Custody Location_, and eventually _Item Returned_ or _Holding Period Expired_.

|*Claim Verification*
|Behavior of _Claim Attempt_, comparison of identifying information, and decision to either allow _Item Returned_ or deny the claim.

|*Matching*
|Behavior of comparing between descriptions of a _Lost Item_ and _Found Item_, potentially leading to a _Claim Attempt_.

|*Custody Management*
|Behavior including receiving items, storing them at a _Custody Location_, monitoring the _Holding Period_ and determining status as _Unclaimed Item_.
|===

===== Type: Function & Phase: Domain

[col="1,3"]
|===
|Term |Definition

|*Reporting*
|Action of documenting the existence of a lost or found item.
|===

===== Type: Function & Phase: Requirements

[col="1,3"]
|===
|Term |Definition

|*Notification*
|Mechanism used to informs relevant individuals about events such as matches, status changes, or recovery progress.

|*Authentication*
|Mechanism used to confirm the identity of a person interacting with the system before allowing restricted actions.
|===
