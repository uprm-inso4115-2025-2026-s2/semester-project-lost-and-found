=== *Terminology*

This section defines key concepts within the lost-and-found domain. 
Each term is annotated with its **type** (entity, event, behavior, function) and the **phase** in which it primarily appears (domain, requirements, implementation).

==== Type= Entity & Phase= Domain 

- **Lost Item**: a personal belonging whose owner no longer knows its location and intends to recover it.
- **Found Item**: a personal belonging discovered by someone other than its owner, with the possibility of being returned.
- **Report**: a recorded description of a lost or found item, including relevant details that support identification and recovery.
- **Owner**: the individual who possesses legitimate claim over a lost item.
- **Finder**: the individual who discovers a lost item and may enable its return to the owner.

==== Type= Event & Phase= Domain

- **Recovery**: the moment in which a lost item is successfully returned to its owner.
- **Closure**: the conclusion of a report’s lifecycle, either because the item was recovered or further recovery is no longer expected.

==== Type= Behavior & Phase= Domain

- **Claim Verification**: the process of determining whether a person asserting ownership of an item is legitimately entitled to receive it.
- **Matching**: the act of determining that a reported lost item and a reported found item correspond to the same physical object based on descriptive evidence.
- *Tracking*: the ongoing observation of a reported item’s status from initial report through recovery or closure.

==== Type= Function & Phase= Domain

- **Reporting**: the action of documenting the existence of a lost or found item so that recovery becomes possible.

==== Type= Function & Phase= Requirements

- **Notification**: a system-supported action that informs relevant individuals about events such as matches, status changes, or recovery progress.
- **Authentication**: a mechanism used to confirm the identity of a person interacting with the system before allowing restricted actions.
