===== Introduction

Modern systems are increasingly socio-technical, meaning they combine technology, human interaction, and organizational processes.

Goal-Oriented Requirements Engineering (GORE) focuses on understanding why a system is needed before deciding how it should be built. One of the main frameworks used in GORE is iStar 2.0, which helps developers analyze stakeholder goals, tasks, resources, and dependencies.

A Lost & Found platform at UPRM could benefit from these concepts by improving organization, communication, and user experience.

===== What is GORE?

Goal-Oriented Requirements Engineering (GORE) is a requirements engineering approach focused on identifying stakeholder goals and system motivations.

Instead of only focusing on technical specifications, GORE analyzes:

* User objectives
* Stakeholder interactions
* System purpose
* Decision-making processes

Benefits of GORE include improved communication, better planning, easier identification of requirements, and stronger decision-making.

===== Socio-Technical Systems

A socio-technical system combines technology with human interaction.

Examples include:

* Healthcare systems
* Online shopping platforms
* University management systems
* Lost & Found platforms

The Lost & Found platform is considered socio-technical because it depends on students, administrators, and digital communication working together effectively.

===== What is iStar 2.0?

iStar 2.0 is a goal-modeling framework used in Requirements Engineering to represent stakeholder intentions and dependencies.

It helps analysts understand:

* Who uses the system
* What goals stakeholders have
* How actors interact
* Which resources are necessary
* What dependencies exist

===== Main Concepts of iStar 2.0

Actors:: Entities that participate in the system.

Goals:: Objectives actors want to achieve.

Tasks:: Actions required to achieve goals.

Resources:: Informational or physical elements needed for tasks.

Qualities:: Desired characteristics such as security or usability.

Dependencies:: Relationships where one actor relies on another.

===== Applying iStar to the Lost & Found Platform

The Lost & Found platform includes several actors such as students, administrators, finders, and the notification system.

Students depend on the platform to report and recover items, while administrators depend on moderation tools to maintain safety and organization.

Modeling these relationships helps developers better understand system requirements before implementation.

===== Benefits of Goal Modeling

Goal modeling provides several benefits:

GORE and iStar 2.0 provide powerful tools for understanding socio-technical systems.

==== [Lecture Topic Task]: Goal Refinement and Goal Diagrams
:author: Victoria Miranda Osuna

===== Goal

Analyze how goal refinement and goal diagrams can be used to organize and model the objectives, while maintaining consistency between high level stakeholder goals and lower level operational goals.

===== Proposed Solution

    - Model high level objectives using goal diagrams.

        * Recover lost items successfully.
        * Improve communication between users.
        * Maintain secure claim verification.
        * Increase platform reliability and trust.

    - Identify how lower level goals such as item reporting, notifications, and claim verification contribute to overall platform objectives.

        * Item reporting improves matching accuracy.
        * Notifications keep users informed about recovery updates.
        * Claim verification prevents false ownership claims.
        * Search filters improve recovery efficiency.

    - Analyze potential conflicts between usability, security, and recovery efficiency goals.

        * Faster recovery may reduce verification accuracy.
        * Strong verification steps may slow down recovery.
        * Simplified reporting forms may collect insufficient information.
        * Frequent notifications may overwhelm users.

    - Apply why and how questioning techniques to refine platform goals into smaller assignable sub goals.

        * Why recover items quickly? → Improve user satisfaction.
        * How to recover items quickly? → Use automated notifications.
        * Why verify ownership? → Prevent fraudulent claims.
        * How to verify ownership? → Require item descriptions or proof images.

===== Success Criteria

* Goal refinements clearly represent relationships between high level and low level platform objectives.
* Goal diagrams correctly demonstrate alternative refinement paths and system options.
* Potential conflicts between platform goals are identified and documented.
* The analysis shows how refined sub goals contribute to effective item recovery and user experience.
